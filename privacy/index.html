Closer by Day Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Closer by Day ("Closer by Day", "we", "us", "the app") is published by Cem Ozcelik, an individual developer, who is the data controller responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: support@closerbyday.com.

This policy explains what the app collects, why, who helps us process it, how long it is kept, and what you can do about it. The short version: the app collects only what it needs to work, it does not track you, and it has no ads and no analytics.

1. What the app collects

Your account. You sign in with Apple. Closer by Day receives an anonymous Apple user identifier and creates an account id for you. We do not ask for your name or email address. If Apple shares a relayed email address, we do not use it.

Your goals and progress. The goals you write, your answers when setting a goal (where you are starting from, what gets in the way, time per step, an optional note, an optional "finish line" sentence), your steps, which days you completed a step, weekly reviews, badges, the optional reason when you let a goal go, and your reminder and AI settings. These are stored on your device and backed up to your account so you can restore them.

AI-written content. The plans, steps, tips and weekly notes the AI writes for you, and the picture subject chosen for each goal.

Purchases. Whether you have an active trial or subscription, which plan, and when it ends. Payments are handled by Apple; Closer by Day never sees your payment details.

Usage counters. How many AI requests your account made and when, so that fair-use limits and cost limits work.

Kept only on your device (never uploaded): your own goal photo if you choose one (stored without location or camera details), your "why it matters" note, and your reminder times.

What the app does not collect: your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos from your library (other than the one photo you pick), advertising identifier, or analytics about how you use the app. The app does not track you across other apps or websites.

2. Why we use it

  • To run the app: your account, your goals, syncing and restoring your data (contract).
  • To write your plans, steps, tips and notes with AI, only if you choose "Build my plan" and agree to AI use (your consent; you can turn AI off at any time in Settings > Data and AI).
  • To check your subscription and unlock features (contract).
  • To prevent abuse and keep AI costs under control, using the usage counters (legitimate interest).

3. AI processing

When you use AI, the app sends to the Closer by Day server, and the server sends to the AI provider: your goal text, your answers listed above, your finish-line sentence and note, and, for later weeks of a weekly goal, the text of the steps you have finished. Your name, account id and device details are not sent to the AI provider.

  • Text AI: Anthropic (Claude API) writes plans, steps, tips and weekly notes.
  • Pictures: goal pictures come from a shared library of illustrations. They are not made from your data and are not unique to you. If a new illustration is ever needed, Google (Gemini API) receives only a short scene description chosen from the app's own fixed list of subjects, never your text.

Under their current terms, these providers do not use the data they receive through their APIs to train their models. Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, and Google keeps Gemini API requests for up to 55 days for abuse monitoring only, except where they must keep data longer by law or to enforce their usage policies.

AI can make mistakes. Plans and notes are suggestions, not professional advice.

5. How long data is kept

  • Your account data is kept until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, your goals, progress, AI content, purchase status and usage counters are deleted from the Closer by Day server, your Sign in with Apple link is revoked, and your RevenueCat customer record is deleted. Apple keeps its own purchase records as required by law.
  • Shared library illustrations stay in the library because they are not your data; the link between your account and a picture is deleted.
  • Server logs contain only technical request information and error codes (no goal text) and are kept for a short period (at most 30 days).
  • Data on your device is removed when you delete the app.

6. Your rights and choices

You can, at any time and even without an active subscription:

  • Export your data: Settings > Data and AI > Export my data (a file you can save or share).
  • Delete your account: Settings > Data and AI > Delete account.
  • Turn off AI: Settings > Data and AI. Nothing more is sent to the AI providers after that.
  • Turn off reminders: in the app or in iOS Settings.

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK), you also have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your data, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. To use any of these rights, email support@closerbyday.com. We will answer within 30 days.

7. Children

The app is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the account.

8. Security

Data is sent only over encrypted connections. Each account can only access its own data. AI keys and other secrets are kept only on the server. No method is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app. If a change affects what is sent to AI, the app asks for your agreement again.
